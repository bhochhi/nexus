# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must help maintain a respectful, inclusive, and professional
project. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, intimidation,
discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not.

Examples of expected behavior include giving actionable feedback, respecting
different experiences and viewpoints, accepting responsibility for mistakes,
and keeping technical discussions focused on the work.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, project-managed communication channels, and public representation of
the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not place sensitive reports in a public issue.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against a person
who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
